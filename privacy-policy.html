Stages Official Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy for Stages

Last updated: September 6, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Stages ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, enjoyable card gaming experience.

2. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

Stages is designed to operate with minimal data access:

  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or password.
  • No Identity Tracking: We do not track, profile, or sell your personal information or device IDs.
  • Microphone Permission (Optional): The app requests microphone access solely to enable optional, real-time voice chat with other players during multiplayer games ("Play with Friends" and "Play Online"). Audio is transmitted in real time and is never recorded, stored, or uploaded to permanent servers.
  • No Location, Contacts, or Photos: The app does not access your GPS location, camera, contacts, or photo library.

3. Multiplayer Rooms, Live Voice Chat & Peer-to-Peer Networking

When you play in a Multiplayer Room:

  • Multiplayer rooms use direct peer-to-peer (WebRTC) encrypted connections via PeerJS.
  • Game actions (card draws, card lays, discards, and room codes) and real-time voice audio are exchanged directly between connected players.
  • Voice audio is strictly ephemeral in transit: it is processed only in active device memory during live speech and is discarded immediately. No voice recordings or gameplay logs are retained on external servers.
  • Players maintain full control and can mute their microphone or speaker at any time using in-game controls.

4. Version 10 Features & Local Processing

Version 10 adds official tournament scoring rules, duplicate-set validation safeguards, colorblind-friendly suit indicators, game-state persistence, card-action spotlights, private draw-card backs, dealing and shuffle animations, and live reaction emotes. These visual effects, sounds, haptics, and reaction controls run locally on your device. Reaction emotes and gameplay actions are sent only to players in the active multiplayer room so the game can display them, and are never used to identify or profile you.

5. Device Storage & Local Preferences

The app uses local device storage (e.g., standard browser LocalStorage or app cache) solely to preserve your in-game preferences, such as your chosen player nickname, audio mute toggle, AI speed setting, tutorial completion flag, and selected visual theme. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted to third parties.

6. Third-Party Services & Android Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Required to allow WebRTC peer connections for Multiplayer Room games. Offline games against computer bots do not transmit network data.
  • RECORD_AUDIO / MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used exclusively for the optional in-game live voice chat feature during multiplayer room matches. Voice chat is strictly opt-in and activated only when the player explicitly enables the microphone.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide subtle tactile/haptic feedback during card deals, draws, discards, and stage completions.

7. Children's Privacy

Stages is suitable for general audiences and families. Because we do not collect or solicit personal information from any user, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families policy requirements.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or data privacy inquiries regarding Stages, you may contact our development support team directly at support@stagescardgame.com.
