Stages Official Privacy Notice

Privacy Policy for Stages

Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Stages ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, enjoyable card gaming experience.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Stages is designed to operate with minimal data access:

  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or password.
  • No Identity Tracking: We do not track, profile, or sell your personal information or device IDs.
  • No Location or Sensor Data: The app does not access your GPS location, microphone, camera, contacts, or photo library.

3. Multiplayer Rooms & Peer-to-Peer Networking

When you play in a Multiplayer Room:

  • Multiplayer rooms use direct peer-to-peer (WebRTC) connections via PeerJS.
  • Game actions (card draws, card lays, discards, and room codes) are exchanged directly between connected players.
  • No gameplay data, chat, or voice communications are recorded, stored, or processed on permanent external databases.

4. Device Storage & Local Preferences

The app may use local device storage (e.g., standard browser LocalStorage or app cache) solely to preserve your in-game preferences, such as your chosen player nickname, audio mute toggle, card animation speed, and selected visual theme. This information stays on your device and is never transmitted to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services & Network Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required exclusively to allow WebRTC peer connections for Multiplayer Room games. Offline games against computer bots and Pass & Play do not transmit network data.

6. Children's Privacy

Stages is suitable for general audiences and families. Because we do not collect or solicit personal information from any user, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families policy requirements.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the development team through the app's official store listing support channel.
