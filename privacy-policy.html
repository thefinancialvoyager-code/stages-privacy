Stages Official Privacy & Data Notice

Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Last updated: September 2026 • Application: Stages Card Game

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Stages (also referred to as "Stages Card Game"). We are committed to player privacy and ensuring players have complete transparency and control over their accounts and data.

2. User Accounts & Data We Collect

Stages provides flexible gameplay options:

  • Guest Play (No Account Required): You can enjoy single-player matches and join multiplayer tables instantly as a Guest without registration or providing any credentials.
  • Optional Email & Password Account: If you choose to register an account to preserve player stats, level, and friend lists across sessions, we collect:
    • Email Address: Used strictly as your account login identifier.
    • Password: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using SHA-256 before storage; plain-text passwords are never stored or transmitted in plain text.
    • Player Display Name: A custom nickname chosen by you for display on leaderboards and in-game tables.
    • Friend Code: A randomly assigned unique identifier (e.g., STG-XXXX) to let friends invite each other to private tables.
  • Optional Google Play Games: Players can connect with their public GamerTag for player recognition.
  • No Personal Tracking: We do not collect GPS location, contacts, device IMEI, or browsing activity.

3. Real-Time Peer-to-Peer Voice Chat & Networking

Multiplayer tables utilize encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer data channels. Optional voice chat is strictly opt-in: audio is transmitted ephemerally in real time and is never recorded, uploaded to permanent storage, or shared with third parties.

4. Account and Data Deletion Policy

In full compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

Option A: In-App Account Deletion (Instant)

  1. Open the Stages app on your device.
  2. From the main lobby or in-game top bar, tap the Player Profile / Sign In button.
  3. Tap "Delete Acct" (or "Clear Data").
  4. Confirm the prompt.

Outcome: Your account credentials, email, password hash, match statistics, rankings, and friend connections are immediately and permanently erased.

Option B: Web / Email Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your device, you can submit a deletion request via email:

  • Send an email to: thefinancialvoyager@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Stages
  • Include your registered email address or player friend code (STG-XXXX).

Timeline: Deletion requests received by email are verified and completely purged within 48 business hours.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained:

  • Deleted: Email address, hashed password, username, friends list, stats, and match records.
  • Retained: None. No user data is retained after deletion.

5. Security & Encryption

All communication with backend services is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS and secure WebSocket (WSS) protocols.

6. Children's Privacy

Stages complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Real-time peer-to-peer voice chat is strictly age-gated and restricted to players aged 13 and older.

7. Developer Contact

For any questions or data inquiries regarding Stages, please contact us at: thefinancialvoyager@gmail.com.
