Stages Official Privacy & Data Notice

Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Last updated: October 2026 • Application: Stages Card Game

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Stages (also referred to as "Stages Card Game"). It explains the device-local player profile, multiplayer connections, and the controls available for local data.

2. Device-Local Player Profiles

  • No Stages online account: Stages does not offer email registration, password login, cloud profiles, or a hosted account database.
  • Local profile data: Your display name, avatar, friend code, game statistics, and preferences are stored in the app data on your device.
  • Shared with players in your room: When you host or join a "With Friends" room, your display name, avatar, and friend code are sent to the other players in that room so they can see who is playing. They are not sent to the developer.
  • Manual profile transfer: A transfer code can contain your local profile, statistics, and friends. The code has a corruption check but is not encrypted, so treat it as private.
  • No personal tracking: We do not collect GPS location, contacts, device IMEI, or browsing activity.

3. Real-Time Peer-to-Peer Multiplayer Networking

"With Friends" private rooms use encrypted WebRTC peer-to-peer data channels strictly for card game state synchronization. There are no public tables or matchmaking with strangers: players can only join a room by entering its 4-letter code. Stages does not use the microphone or camera and does not record or stream audio.

To connect devices, Stages uses third-party networking services: the public PeerJS signaling server (0.peerjs.com) and STUN servers operated by Google (stun.l.google.com) and Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com). These services receive your device's IP address and temporary connection identifiers while a room is open. They do not receive your cards, statistics, or friends list.

4. Clearing Local Profile Data

Open Privacy from the main menu and tap Clear Local Data to remove your profile and statistics from this device.

Because Stages has no hosted account database, there is no server-side Stages account to delete and the developer cannot remotely recover or erase data stored only on your device.

5. Security & Networking

Multiplayer game state synchronizes using encrypted WebRTC data connections. A signaling service helps peers connect but is not a Stages account or profile service.

6. Children's Privacy

Stages complies with COPPA and Google Play Families policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
