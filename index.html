Stages Official Privacy & Data Notice

Privacy Policy & Account Deletion

Last updated: October 2026 (Stages 2.20.0) • Application: Stages Card Game

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Stages (also called "Stages Card Game"), published on Google Play by Voyge Interactive ("we", "us"). Contact: thefinancialvoyager@gmail.com.

We do not run a Stages account system or a game server. Most information stays on your phone. In friend games, some information goes directly to the other players' phones, and the network and Google services named below process some data on their own systems under their own privacy policies. Stages shows no advertising and contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

2. Stored on your phone

  • Your profile: display name, avatar, friend code (a random code that identifies your profile to friends), game statistics and settings.
  • A saved game against the computer, so it can be resumed after Android closes the app.
  • Friend-game safety settings: your month and year of birth (asked before your first friend game), whether you accepted the Terms of Service, the players you blocked (their names and friend codes), and whether Stages may use your Google Play Games name.
  • This stays in the app's storage until you tap Clear Local Data or uninstall Stages. Clearing creates a fresh default profile with a new friend code so the app keeps working.
  • Not collected: Stages does not access your precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos or camera. It uses the microphone only for the optional voice chat in section 6, and only after you turn it on.

3. No Stages account, no ads, no analytics

Stages has no email registration, password login or account database. It shows no ads, does not use your device's advertising ID, and sends no data to ad networks or analytics services.

4. Friend games (private rooms)

"With Friends" rooms are private: there are no public tables or matchmaking with strangers. A friend joins by entering the room's 6-character code or opening its QR link, and the host can lock the room once everyone is in.

  • What the other players in your room receive: your display name, avatar, friend code, temporary player and connection identifiers, your moves, the reactions you send, and whether you are in voice chat or muted.
  • The host's phone runs the game, so it receives the full game state, including every player's cards. Other players' phones receive only what the game shows them.
  • IP addresses: the connections go directly between phones (encrypted WebRTC), so the other players in your room can see your device's IP address, as with any voice or video calling app.
  • Room information is kept in the phones' memory while the room is open and is not stored afterwards.

5. Network services

To connect phones, Stages uses the public PeerJS signaling server (0.peerjs.com) and STUN servers run by Google (stun.l.google.com) and Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com). While a room is open they receive your device's IP address, temporary connection identifiers and connection timing. They do not receive your cards, statistics or voice. Each service decides how long it keeps its own server logs; we do not receive those logs.

Joining with a QR code: the host's QR code links to a page on this website (thefinancialvoyager-code.github.io), hosted by GitHub Pages. When a friend opens it, GitHub receives their IP address and browser details, and the link contains the room code. The page stores nothing; its button only passes the room code to Stages on that phone. Stages itself does not use the camera: the friend scans the code with their phone's own camera app.

These providers, and the other players in a room, may be in other countries than you.

6. Voice Chat in Friend Games (Optional)

During a "With Friends" game you can talk with the other players in your room. Voice chat is off until you tap the Voice button.

  • Microphone permission: The first time you turn voice chat on, Android asks whether Stages may use your microphone. You can say no and keep playing without voice, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's Settings › Apps › Stages › Permissions.
  • Where your voice goes: Your voice is streamed live, directly from your device to the devices of the other players in your room who have also turned voice chat on, over encrypted WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP). It does not pass through any server run by us, and the services in section 5 do not receive it.
  • Not recorded or stored: Stages does not record, save, upload, transcribe or analyze what you say. The only processing is on your device: echo and noise reduction, and a volume check that lights up the "talking" indicator.
  • Who knows you are in voice: Other players in the room can see whether you are in voice chat and whether your microphone is muted.
  • Your controls: You can mute your microphone, mute everyone, mute a single player for yourself, block a player, or turn voice chat off at any time. Your microphone is switched off while Stages is in the background, and voice chat ends when you leave the game.
  • Other players: Stages cannot stop another player from recording their own device. Only talk with people you trust, and do not share personal information over voice chat.

7. Google Play Games (Optional)

  • When Google Play Games is available on your phone, its library checks with Google Play services when Stages starts, so you stay signed in if you signed in before. Google then tells Stages your Play Games display name and player ID.
  • Stages does not store or use the player ID.
  • Stages uses your Play Games display name only after you agree: by tapping Sign in in Settings › Account, or Use my Play Games name on the notice Stages shows the first time. It is then saved on your phone and, if you kept the default name "Player", becomes your name at the table, which the players in your friend rooms see.
  • Tap Unlink in Settings › Account at any time: Stages forgets the Play Games name and stops using it. To sign out of Google Play Games or delete your Play Games profile, use the Google Play Games app or your Google Account; that data is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Every part of the game works without Google Play Games.

8. Reports and blocks

  • Block: blocking a player mutes their voice for you, keeps them out of rooms you host, and makes Stages leave rooms they host. Your block list is kept only on your phone (by friend code) and can be undone in the Privacy screen.
  • Report: reporting a player opens your own email app with a message to us containing the reason you picked, the reported player's name and friend code, the room code, your friend code, the time and the app version. Nothing is sent unless you press Send, and you can edit the message first. We use reports only to review behaviour and enforce the Terms of Service, and we delete report emails within 12 months unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

9. Age

Friend games and voice chat are for players aged 13 and over. Before your first friend game, Stages asks for your month and year of birth; the answer stays on your phone. Players under 13 can still play against the computer, which keeps everything on the phone. If you believe a child under 13 has used friend games, contact us and we will help remove their data from the device.

10. Deleting Your Data

On your phone: Open Privacy from the main menu and tap Clear Local Data to remove your profile, statistics, saved game, safety settings, block list and the stored copy of your Play Games name from this phone. Uninstalling Stages also removes all of its data from your phone.

Held by Google: If you use Google Play Games, your Play Games profile can be deleted in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account (section 7).

Because Stages has no account database of its own, there is no Stages account on a server to delete. Voice chat is never stored. Clearing your data does not remove anything another player may have copied or recorded on their own device, or report emails you sent us (section 8), which you can ask us to delete.

11. Your choices and rights

Most Stages data exists only on your phone or on the phones of the players in your room, so we cannot see or change it; Clear Local Data deletes your copy. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data we hold (in practice, report emails) and to complain to a data-protection authority. Write to thefinancialvoyager@gmail.com with any request or question; this address is also our contact for privacy grievances.

12. Security

Friend-game moves and voice chat are exchanged over encrypted WebRTC connections. Connections to Google Play Games use encrypted HTTPS. Stages checks every message from another phone before using it and limits how much a phone may send.

13. Changes to This Policy

If we change how Stages handles data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect in the app.
